## Positioning

Carry the sales conversation from the business card to the website. Help local
owners see how customers can find them, check what they do and where they work,
and send an inquiry the owner can answer when free. Lead with websites and
practical marketing advice. Add case studies and dedicated service pages after
launch as demand warrants.

## Tickets and acceptance

1. **Sales copy.** Lead with “Make it easier to hire you” and invite owners to
   “Tell me about your business.” Replace the DIY argument with the electrician
   story and the practical fix. Align About and page search descriptions.
2. **Offer.** Review with ticket 1 in the same homepage revision. Publish
   Starter at $500 with core-page copy and Search Console and sitemap setup,
   Sell or book at $1,200 for one standard flow, Custom from $2,500, and
   maintenance at $50/month for updates, backups, and small edits. Quote new
   pages and features separately.
3. **Assets and contact.** Publish all five pages with approved team portraits
   and the GrannyCoded screenshot. Verify mobile rendering, saved inquiries,
   and real phone links. Finish before the transfer.
4. **Production transfer.** Confirm the host, domain, and empty database.
   Deploy the approved revision with production settings, runtime, and static
   assets. Review the laptop database and restore its dump with matching media.
5. **Cutover.** Before opening traffic, verify HTTPS, pages, mobile rendering,
   images, pricing, sitemap, phone, and an inquiry saved in Wagtail and received
   by email. Repeat homepage and contact checks on the public domain. Make
   production Wagtail the source for live edits.
